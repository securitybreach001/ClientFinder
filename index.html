How the score works

Each year since the earliest corroborated licence earns one point, up to 40. A checked business with no website found earns 60 additional points. A confirmed website earns zero additional points. Scores with missing history or website evidence are provisional. The score ranks outreach opportunities and the licence year reflects available records.
